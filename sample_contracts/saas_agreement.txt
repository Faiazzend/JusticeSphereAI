SECTION 1. SUBSCRIPTION AND AUTO-RENEWAL
1.1 Auto-Renewal Lock-In. The subscription term shall automatically renew for successive two-year periods unless Customer provides written notice of non-renewal at least 180 days prior to expiration. Fees increase by 10% per renewal term.

SECTION 2. TERMINATION AND CURE
2.1 Default. In the event of breach, Vendor may effect immediate termination without any cure period.

SECTION 3. SERVICE CREDITS
3.1 Exclusive Remedy. SLA credits shall be the exclusive financial remedy for downtime.
