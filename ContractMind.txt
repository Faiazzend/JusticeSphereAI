ContractMind
Scored
Contract intelligence and automation

Specific Ask

Deliver a functional AI-native prototype that can ingest a raw contract PDF, perform automated risk scoring based on custom playbooks, and output a structured JSON of obligations alongside a redlined version of the document.

Details

ContractMind challenges builders to overhaul the archaic legal document lifecycle using AI-native workflows that move beyond simple OCR. Teams should leverage multi-agent systems and RAG architectures to automate complex contract redlining, risk discovery, and obligation extraction, transforming static PDFs into dynamic, actionable intelligence. The expected solution uses LLMs for semantic reasoning and vision models to interpret structural nuances in complex legal formatting.

Example Prompts

Build a multi-agent system where one agent acts as 'opposing counsel' to identify high-risk indemnification clauses while another suggests commercially viable counter-offers.
Develop a RAG-based engine that cross-references a new MSA against a company's historical 'Gold Standard' clauses to highlight deviations and missing protections.
Create a vision-based agent that parses complex hierarchical tables in financial contracts to build a structured payment milestone calendar automatically.
Implement an automated redlining tool that utilizes chain-of-thought prompting to explain the legal rationale behind every suggested text modification.
Design a natural language interface that allows non-legal stakeholders to query a repository of 5,000+ contracts for specific liability caps using semantic search.
Data Sources

EDGAR Database: The SEC's public repository containing millions of real-world corporate contracts and material agreements.
CUAD v1: A comprehensive dataset of 500+ curated legal contracts with 13,000+ expert annotations for contract review tasks.
MAUD (Merger Agreement Understanding Dataset): A specialized dataset for evaluating LLM performance on complex legal reasoning in M&A.
Open Legal Data: A platform providing access to diverse European court decisions and legal documents for cross-jurisdictional training.
Common Crawl (Legal Subset): Massive web-crawl data filtered for legal professional forums, templates, and public policy documents.
Examples

Ironclad: A digital contracting platform that uses AI to accelerate the negotiation process and manage contract repositories.
Luminance: An AI platform that uses supervised and unsupervised machine learning to read and understand legal documents.
Spellbook: A GPT-4 powered tool that integrates directly into Microsoft Word to assist lawyers in drafting and reviewing contracts.
Kira Systems: Machine learning software that identifies, extracts, and analyzes content in contracts and other documents.
Lawgeex: An AI-powered contract review automation platform designed to ensure compliance with company policies.
